About this recipe

Refresh your palate with this vibrant Summer Quinoa Salad. It's a perfect balance of sweet and savory, featuring protein-rich quinoa, crisp seasonal vegetables, and a zesty citrus dressing. Ideal for meal prepping or a light lunch on a sunny afternoon.

Quick Tip

Toast the quinoa for 2-3 minutes in the pan before adding water to unlock a deeper, nuttier flavor profile.
